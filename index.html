Shadow Theatre

Turn on your camera and the room becomes a lit screen. The four creatures are rigged like little 2D actors — they breathe, blink, and copy the people in front of the camera. Tilt your head, lean, raise an arm and they do the same. Pick one up, fling it, and it swings on its strings.

  • Follow mode — up to four people, one monster each (one person gets all four)
  • Puppet mode — wave, pinch and drag to move them yourself
  • Scroll to resize, double-tap to make it spin
  • T hides the camera for a plain black stage — the rigs stay lit

Video never leaves your device — it is only drawn on this page.
